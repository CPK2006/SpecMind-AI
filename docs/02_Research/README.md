# Research

This section stores research that informs SpecMind AI's product, AI, architecture, and delivery decisions.

Research may cover requirements engineering practices, document formats, AI provider behavior, quality heuristics, user feedback, and comparable tools. Each substantial research note should record its question, sources, findings, confidence, and the decision or follow-up it informs.

Do not store provider credentials, private customer documents, or unredacted personal data in this directory.
